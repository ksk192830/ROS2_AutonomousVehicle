# Sofa 04 - 3D PBR Curved Sofa attribution

This work is based on **3D PBR Curved Sofa** by [Mustafa Özgen](https://sketchfab.com/mustafaozgen).

- Modification: Converted the downloadable glTF scene to OBJ, removed the presentation ground plane, transformed it to Gazebo's Z-up frame, centered it at ground level, resized the retained color and normal textures from 2K to 1K, and added a simplified collision box.
