# Sofa 01 - modern sofa attribution

This work is based on **modern sofa** by [abir_sd](https://sketchfab.com/abir_sd).

- Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/modern-sofa-60e1b861407547bca760424c9c38ec78
- License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Modification: Converted the downloadable glTF scene to OBJ, removed the presentation room, transformed it to Gazebo's Z-up frame, centered it at ground level, and added a simplified collision box. The two chairs, center table, books, and sculpture are retained as one furniture set.
